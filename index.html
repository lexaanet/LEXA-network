FAQ

🚀 What is LEXA?
LEXA is a community-powered digital ecosystem where members earn rewards through Mining, Daily Check-in, Referrals, and future ecosystem activities. Our mission is to build a sustainable platform that grows together with its community.
⛏ How does Mining work?
Mining runs for 12 hours per session. Once completed, rewards are automatically added to your balance and you can immediately start the next mining session.
🔥 How does Daily Check-in work?
Claim your Daily Check-in every day to maintain your streak. Every multiple of 7 consecutive days grants an increasing bonus reward. Missing one day resets your streak back to Day 1.
🌍 What is the future of LEXA?
LEXA is currently under active development. Future updates include Marketplace Utility, Community Events, Referral Expansion, Token Utility, and Withdrawal support after the official launch.
🌌 Build the Future with LEXA
Mining is only the beginning. Stay active, grow your network, and become part of the LEXA ecosystem. Powered by Community. Built for the Future.
